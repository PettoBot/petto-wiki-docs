## Overview

Members can **rate each other** from 1 to 5 stars, with an optional comment. A member can review another once, and sending it again changes the review. You can not review yourself or a bot.

`!profile` shows when a member joined, their rating, and the numbers of the modules the server uses: [uploads](/community/uploads), [requests](/community/requests) and [partnerships](/community/partners).

## Commands

<CodeGroup>

```javascript Syntax
!review give <user> <stars> [comment]
!review show [user]
!review remove <user>
!profile [user]
!profileconfig view
!profileconfig reviews <true|false>
!profileconfig channel [channel]
```

```javascript Example
!review give @Mia 5 "Fast and kind"
!review show @Mia
!profile @Mia
!profileconfig channel #reviews
```

</CodeGroup>

The stars can be written as `4`, `4/5` or `4 stars`. With `!profileconfig channel`, every new review is shown in that channel. `!profileconfig reviews false` turns reviews off, and the profile stops showing the rating.

## Dashboard

The **Reviews and profiles** page has the same settings.
