## Overview

A member asks for something with `!request make`. Petto posts a **card** in the channel you choose, with buttons for the staff:

| Button | Who can press it |
| --- | --- |
| **Claim** | The staff |
| **Done** | Who claimed it, or the staff |
| **Unclaim** | Who claimed it, or who manages the server |
| **Cancel** | The author, or the staff |

The staff is the role you choose, or, if there is none, whoever can manage messages. Who manages the server is always staff. When a request is done, Petto tells the author in the same channel.

<Note>
With **Premium**, a claimed request that is not finished within the hours you set goes back to open by itself, and the staff is told.
</Note>

## Commands

<CodeGroup>

```javascript Syntax
!request make <text>
!request list [mine]
!requestconfig view
!requestconfig enable <true|false>
!requestconfig channel <channel>
!requestconfig staff [role]
!requestconfig ping [role]
!requestconfig limit <1-25>
!requestconfig timeout <hours>
!requestconfig reply [text] [template]
```

```javascript Example
!requestconfig enable true
!requestconfig channel #requests
!requestconfig staff @Designers
!request make A banner for my server, pink and blue
```

</CodeGroup>

The answer to who makes a request can be text or one of your [saved embeds](/resources/scripting/embeds), with the `{request.*}` [variables](/resources/scripting/variables). `limit` is how many open requests a member can have at once.

## Dashboard

The **Requests** page has the same settings and the open requests.
