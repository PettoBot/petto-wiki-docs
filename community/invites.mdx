## Overview

Tracks who invited whom, with a leaderboard of who's bringing in the most members.

## Commands

| Command | Syntax | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| invites | `/invites [user]` | See who invited whom, and a per-server leaderboard. |

Purely a tracking/lookup feature, nothing to configure.
