## Overview

A jailed member loses their roles and can only see the jail channel, where they can talk to staff. Roles are saved before anything changes and given back on release.

## Setup

<CodeGroup>

```javascript Syntax
!jail setup
```

</CodeGroup>

Creates the **Jailed** role and the **#jail** channel and hides every other channel from the role. Run it again to repair them. New channels are hidden automatically. Petto needs **Manage Roles** and **Manage Channels**.

## Commands

<CodeGroup>

```javascript Syntax
!jail user <user> [duration] [reason]
!jail remove <user> [reason]
!jail list
!unjail <user> [reason]
```

```javascript Example
!jail user @Someone 2h "Arguing in chat"
!jail remove @Someone "Calmed down"
!unjail @Someone
```

</CodeGroup>

With a `duration` the jail ends by itself and the roles come back. `jail list` shows who is jailed now. Every jail and release creates a [case](/concepts/cases).

## Good to know

- A jailed member who leaves and rejoins is jailed again.
- A role handed to a jailed member by another bot is taken off again and kept for release.
- `jail` can be the action of a [warn escalation](/moderation/moderation#warn-escalation) rule.
