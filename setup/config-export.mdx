## Overview

The [dashboard](https://petto.sbs/dash)'s General page shows a live, read-only snapshot of every module's configuration for your server, including anything not yet set (shown as its real default, not hidden).

## Download

Download the full snapshot as JSON, useful for backups or comparing configuration across servers.

## Reset

Resets the basics back to defaults: prefix, mute role, and bot appearance (nickname/avatar/banner/about me). It does not touch other modules like moderation, automation, or leveling, those are reset individually where they're configured.
